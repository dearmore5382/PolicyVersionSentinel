POLICY_VERSION=REFUND-CONFLICT-2026-01
EFFECTIVE_DATE=2026-01-01

CLAUSE_ID=CONFLICT-1
A refund request submitted within 14 calendar days is eligible when the digital service has not been substantially consumed.

CLAUSE_ID=CONFLICT-2
Every refund request submitted within 14 calendar days is eligible regardless of consumption.

CLAUSE_ID=CONFLICT-3
A substantially consumed digital service is ineligible for a refund, including requests submitted within 14 calendar days.

CLAUSE_ID=CONFLICT-4
When applicable clauses produce opposing outcomes, the request requires manual review and must be classified as ambiguous.
POLICY_END
